# Write

Draft a post or message in the person's voice. Render their words. Do not write your own.

**The guide wins.** Everything below marked "default" applies only where the person's guide and approved drafts say nothing. If their evidence shows long posts, warm openers, lists or exclamation marks, write that way.

## Read first

- **A post:** `~/voice/guide.md`, the matching file in `templates/formats/`, and the two FINAL drafts in `~/voice/drafts/` closest to the format.
- **A message to a friend:** the guide's "Messages to friends" and "Hard bans" sections, and `templates/formats/dm.md`.

## Draft

1. Keep the person's words and their order. Add only what is missing: a fact they gave elsewhere, a link, a caveat they would make.
2. Match the length their approved drafts show for this format. Default, if there are none: cut filler until every line carries something. Never cut their facts.
3. If a word looks like a dictation error, fix the obvious ones and list the doubtful ones under the draft.
4. For a post, run the detox pass in SKILL.md.
5. Check every hard ban in the guide.

## Quality gates

- Every number came from the person or a source.
- Nothing breaks a hard ban in the guide.
- The opening and ending match how their approved drafts open and end. Default: open on something specific, and end without a summary.
- Only this person could have written it. They would say every line out loud.

## Output

Show the draft clean, with at most one variant. Save it to `~/voice/drafts/<format>/<date>-<topic>.md`. When the person approves a version, mark it FINAL. FINAL drafts are the best training data.
