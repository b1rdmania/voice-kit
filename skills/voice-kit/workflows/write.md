# Write

Draft a post or message in the person's voice. Render their words. Do not write your own.

## Read first

- **A post:** `~/voice/guide.md`, the matching file in `templates/formats/`, and the two FINAL drafts in `~/voice/drafts/` closest to the format.
- **A message to a friend:** only the guide's "Messages to friends" and "Hard bans" sections, and `templates/formats/dm.md`.

## Draft

1. Keep the person's words and their order. Cut, don't rephrase. Add only what is missing: a fact they gave elsewhere, a link, an honest caveat.
2. Write at half the length of the first draft you would write. Cut filler, never their facts.
3. If a word looks like a dictation error, fix the obvious ones and list the doubtful ones under the draft.
4. For a post, run the detox pass in SKILL.md.
5. Check every hard ban in the guide.

## Quality gates

- It starts with a specific claim or fact.
- Every number came from the person or a source.
- It ends on the ask, the link or an honest caveat. No summary, no engagement question.
- Only this person could have written it. They would say every line out loud.

## Output

Show the draft clean, with at most one short variant. Save it to `~/voice/drafts/<format>/<date>-<topic>.md`. When the person approves a version, mark it FINAL. FINAL drafts are the best training data.
