# LinkedIn post

## Shapes

- **Launch.** Short lines: what it is, why it matters, one honest line, the ask. Fuller detail and links go in the first comment.
- **Problem post.** A fact from their own history, short lines naming each failure, a one-line turn, the ask.
- **Recap.** A detail that proves they were there, the main read, then specifics with names.

## Rules

The first two lines must work alone, because that is all that shows before "see more". No listicles. No "what do you think?" ending.
