# X post

## Shapes

- **Launch.** A hook (a question they had, a personal fact), what it is in one line, how it works in two to four short lines, the honest state, the link.
- **Claim.** One strong claim and one line of proof.
- **Quote post.** Agree or disagree in a few words, then what they built or saw.

## Rules

Use the guide's capitalisation for X. Short paragraphs. Lists use dashes. One emoji at most. No threads unless the person asks. No hashtags.
