# Detox core

A short fallback for when the `plain-english` skill is not installed. Flag each item, then keep it only if the guide's "Keep anyway" table gives a reason.

1. **Banned words.** delve, tapestry, navigate, leverage, landscape, ecosystem, realm, foster, underscore, robust, comprehensive, nuanced, crucial, pivotal, holistic, seamless, unlock. Use the plain word or cut it.
2. **Em dashes.** None, unless the guide allows them.
3. **No preamble.** Cut openers that frame what follows.
4. **No summary closer.** Cut a last line that restates the post.
5. **No hedge stacks.** One hedge per claim at most. "could potentially" becomes "could".
6. **No padded threes.** Use two or four items when the content has two or four.
7. **One name per thing.** Do not rotate synonyms for the same thing.
8. **No unnamed authority.** "Experts agree" needs a name or goes.
9. **Vary sentence length.** Mix short and long.
10. **No engagement bait.** No "what do you think?" ending.

The full audit, with 24 rules and the reason step, is in the plain-english skill.
