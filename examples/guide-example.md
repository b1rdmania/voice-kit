# Voice guide: @tilly_example (made-up example)

*Built 2026-10-02 from 2,140 messages (Claude Code, ChatGPT export), 96,000 words, plus 4 approved examples. Tilly is not a real person.*

## The one rule

Says what broke before what worked.

## Registers

| Register | Where | What it looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typed | Quick commands, messages to friends | Lowercase, no full stops, "ok", "nah", "ship it" |
| Dictated | Thinking aloud | Long, starts "So basically", lots of "I think" |
| Outbound | Emails to customers | Short, warm, signs off "T" |
| Post | X, LinkedIn | Short lines, numbers first, one honest caveat |

Posts take the substance of the dictated register and the form of the outbound one.

## How a post sounds

Opens with a number or a failure: "Lost 40% of trials in week one." States numbers bare. Admits the gap in the same breath as the win. Lands on a short flat line: "So we cut it." Ends on the ask or the link.

## Humour

Dry, aimed at herself. "Shipped onboarding v3. Users found a way to skip it in four minutes. Respect."

## Describing her own work

Numbers, no adjectives. "Churn down from 9% to 6%. Still too high."

## Vocabulary

- **Current:** ship, cut, kinda, honestly (once), "still too high".
- **Chat only:** nah, ship it, lol.
- **Retired:** "growth hacking".

## Hard bans

- No exclamation marks. ("take the exclamation marks out, I'm not a kids TV host")
- No "excited to share". ("never say excited")
- No bold text in posts. ("why is half of this bold")

## Keep anyway

| Flag | Keep it when | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fragments | Any post | rhythm |
| "kinda" | Once per post | voice |

## Messages to friends

Under 30 words. The plan first, then "you in?".

> pub at 7 after the demo. you in? need someone to tell me it went fine
